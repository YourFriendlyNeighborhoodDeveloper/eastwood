AI outbound sales · insurance · compliance-first

A voice agent that sells insurance by the rules, and won't make things up.

Eastwood takes purchased or inbound leads, decides who may lawfully be called and who is worth calling, then has Imogen, a realtime AI voice assistant, run the discovery call. She screens health, presents deterministic quotes, and books the prospect with a licensed human agent who closes. Every step is gated by code, not by asking the model nicely.

How it works

From a row in a spreadsheet to a booked appointment

Each lead passes through the same pipeline. The green steps are hard gates: if one fails, the lead stops there, and nothing downstream can override it.

  1. Ingest

    CSV or PDF lead exports with fuzzy column mapping, an HTTP ingest API, signed Facebook Lead Ads webhooks, and web forms.

  2. Normalize

    Phones to E.164, product lines, age from DOB, and consent records. Unmapped columns are kept for audit.

  3. Compliance gates

    TCPA 8am–9pm local window, National DNC clearance (fail-closed), prior opt-outs, express written consent.

  4. Triage & score

    Medical knockouts, severity scoring, wealth and fit signals, then a route: auto-dial, request consent, nurture, or suppress.

  5. Dial queue

    A concurrency cap, a 4/24/32/56/80-hour retry ladder, max attempts, and idempotent handling of Twilio status callbacks.

  6. Imogen's call

    Discloses she is an AI, confirms identity, runs needs discovery and health screening, and captures only what she clearly heard.

  7. Quote

    Unlocks only after discovery is complete. The engine picks three options; the model only explains them.

  8. Book & hand off

    A real Calendly booking, a full prospect profile persisted, and an SMS brief to the licensed agent. Outcome-aware follow-ups run after.

The voice agent

Meet Imogen: silence is never an answer

Speech-to-speech models predict the likeliest next line of a sales call, which is "Perfect, thanks" followed by the next question, even when the prospect only coughed. Eastwood takes the decision to speak away from voice detection and rules on what was actually said first.

Illustrative excerpt · turn gate in action
IMOGEN
And do you currently use any tobacco or nicotine products?
PROSPECT
[half-second of line noise]
turn_gate → not_heard · noise_only · slot tobacco stays UNKNOWN · "Perfect, thanks" blocked
IMOGEN
Sorry, I didn't catch that. Was that a yes or a no?
PROSPECT
No, quit years ago.
transcript final · confidence 0.94 · answers yes_no → slot tobacco CONFIRMED = "no"
IMOGEN
Good to know. Have you been diagnosed with any heart conditions in the last two years?

Three-state slots

Every answer moves UNKNOWN → HEARD → CONFIRMED. Only confirmed values reach the quote engine or the handoff brief. "Unknown" is an acceptable outcome; a wrong value is not.

Transcript grounding

To store a value, the model must quote what it heard. That quote is matched against finalized transcripts of the prospect's own speech and scored with per-token logprob confidence.

A discovery state machine

Purpose → beneficiary → burial or cremation → coverage → rating sex → tobacco → health. Code owns which question is next; the persona owns the wording.

Turn-taking that holds up on a real phone line

Barge-in cancels speech and flushes queued audio. A watchdog nudges a stalled model, then ends the call cleanly. After three unheard turns she names the line problem instead of looping.

Capabilities

Grouped by subsystem. Every item below exists in the code today; the core paths are covered by the 453 automated tests.

01 · Intake

Lead ingestion

  • Any vendor CSV, with fuzzy column-to-field mapping
  • PDF lead exports (tables extracted page by page)
  • POST /api/leads/ingest HTTP API
  • Facebook Lead Ads webhook, signature-verified
  • Browser lead uploader and a 60-second "risk scan" funnel
  • Lead batches tracked by vendor and cost for ROI
02 · Scoring

Enrichment, scoring & routing

  • Optional OpenFEC donor and NPI provider lookups
  • Home-value wealth tiers and per-product age-fit bands
  • Consent freshness, state fit, recent-touch penalty
  • Hot / warm / cold bands with configurable thresholds
  • Routes to auto_dial, request_consent_sms, nurture, manual_review, or suppress
03 · Triage

Medical field underwriting

  • L0 compliance and contactability gate
  • L1 universal knockouts: hospice, dementia, ALS, and more
  • Guaranteed-issue floor conditions
  • Severity scoring with comorbidity bumps and lookback windows
  • Medication proxies (insulin → diabetes, Eliquis → cardiac)
  • CALL_NOW / WORKABLE / DEAD buckets, a versioned ruleset
04 · Placement

Carrier placement engine

  • Ranks carriers for each lead and the rate tier each would likely offer: level, graded, modified, or GI
  • Lists the exact questions still needed per carrier
  • 9 profiles: American Amicable, Mutual of Omaha, Royal Neighbors, Transamerica, Prosperity Life, Aetna / CVS, Humana
05 · Voice

Imogen, the realtime voice agent

  • OpenAI Realtime speech-to-speech over Twilio Media Streams
  • Discloses she is an AI up front and whenever asked
  • Tier-1 screening, plus Tier-2 follow-ups added when a condition is confirmed
  • Turn gate, slot state, transcript grounding, barge-in, watchdog
  • Six product scripts: final expense, life, annuity, Medicare Advantage, Medigap, ACA
06 · Tools

12 tools, unlocked by data

  • update_lead_field, add_followup_questions
  • get_quote, select_quote_option
  • get_booking_slots, book_appointment
  • capture_consent, capture_objection, capture_dnc
  • transfer_live, transfer_to_human, end_call
  • Availability is derived from what's been captured. After a do-not-call request, only end_call remains.
07 · Quoting

Deterministic three-option quotes

  • Engine selects options C / B / A (minimum, recommended, maximum within budget)
  • Adapters: CMS Medicare data API, carrier rate tables, formula estimates
  • Honesty labels on every quote: quote_type, rate_source, carrier_rate, bindable
  • Carrier name hidden on estimates
  • Premium-per-$1k sanity bands veto implausible quotes
08 · Guardrails

Live policy engine

  • Blocks "guaranteed", "can't be denied", "no questions asked"
  • Blocks implying Medicare plans are a government benefit (CMS)
  • Blocks reading back SSNs or bank and routing numbers
  • Blocks vague "$60 to $80" ranges once exact quotes exist
  • Every decision counted in agent_policy_decisions_total
09 · Handoff

Booking & licensed-agent handoff

  • Calendly Scheduling API: offers real availability and books the slot
  • Idempotent bookings, with honest failure if Calendly is down
  • Full prospect profile persisted in handoff_profiles
  • SMS brief: name, time, purpose, beneficiary, budget, health, quote, motivation, objections, open questions
  • Outcome SMS after every connected call; a live-transfer alert for hot prospects
10 · Follow-up

Outcome-aware sequences

  • Missed call, voicemail left, interested-but-busy, no contact
  • Quoted-not-closed, price objection, spouse objection
  • Application not completed, payment failed
  • Mixes SMS and callbacks with personalized templates
  • A do-not-call request cancels every pending touch
11 · Compliance

Regulatory controls

  • TCPA calling window per state time zone (all 50 states + DC)
  • Official FTC National DNC dataset: checksummed, freshness-limited, SAN stored only as a fingerprint
  • Opt-out suppressions honored on re-import
  • Consent evidence packs with SHA-256 hashes
  • Boot guards: production refuses to start in an unsafe configuration
12 · Operations

Operator APIs & observability

  • Closer cockpit: best-first queue with placement and a suggested opener
  • Batch ROI and lead-source ROI, computed live
  • Call-outcome tagging and a dashboard summary
  • Prometheus + Grafana, per-turn latency reports, call tracing
  • Answering-machine detection and an abandoned-session reaper
Boundaries

What it does, and what it deliberately doesn't

The right column matters as much as the left. Most of these refusals are enforced in code, at the point where the action would happen.

✓ Eastwood does

  • Pre-qualify and educate. Discovery, health screening, and carrier fit before a human spends a minute.
  • Present real numbers it can stand behind. Deterministic options, labeled as estimates or carrier rates.
  • Book the licensed agent, with a complete, factual brief and a persisted profile.
  • Honor opt-outs instantly. A mid-call "stop calling" suppresses the number and cancels redials.
  • Keep evidence. Consent text verbatim, hashed evidence packs, a field-level capture log.
  • Measure itself. Turn latency, gate verdicts, tool vetoes, session leaks, duplicate events.

✕ Eastwood does not

  • Sell, bind, or apply. Imogen is not licensed. No quote is bindable, no application is submitted, and no payment is taken.
  • Pretend to be human. She opens as an AI and answers truthfully if asked.
  • Dial without clearance. No call outside 8am–9pm local, to any number not explicitly clear or exempt on the DNC registry, or without express written consent.
  • Invent answers. Unheard or unclear means the slot stays unknown, never a guess.
  • Collect sensitive identifiers. No SSNs and no bank details, on the call or in read-backs.
  • Promise approval or imply Medicare plans are a government benefit.
  • Name a carrier on an estimate, or quote before discovery is complete.
  • Bridge live transfers automatically. A hot prospect triggers an SMS alert for the closer to call in.
  • Handle inbound calls, serve multiple tenants, or ship an operator UI. It's outbound-only and single-tenant, with JSON APIs and Grafana.
